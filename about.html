Background

From mechanical design to control and automation

I come from Lebanon, where I studied mechanical engineering at Beirut Arab University. My coursework covered mechanical design, thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, heat transfer, system dynamics and control theory, and my final-year project tested an ethanol–diesel dual-fuel engine.

After graduating I worked as a Mechanical Design Engineer at My Projects SAL. I calculated thermal loads, sized pumps, fans, compressors and heat exchangers, prepared BOQs and AutoCAD shop drawings, and supervised installation on site. That gave me a practical feel for fluids, heat transfer and how equipment behaves once it is installed.

I then moved to Italy for an M.Sc. in Automation & Control Systems Engineering at the University of Padova, where I also programmed Siemens PLCs in TIA Portal and commissioned them virtually against Factory I/O plants. My thesis developed the control of an electrically excited synchronous motor for a heavy-duty IVECO vehicle, covering field-oriented control, MTPA, flux weakening and MTPV, with Extremum Seeking Control cutting simulated Joule losses by up to 10%.

Since April 2025 I have worked at STIGA on autonomous electric robotic platforms: bringing up prototype chassis, integrating motors, drives and sensors, and analysing closed-loop motor behaviour from CAN data.
